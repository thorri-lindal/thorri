## 🔥 Notable People in Cybersecurity

- **Sparc Flow** – Author of *Red Teaming* and expert in offensive security.
- **[Kevin Mitnick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Mitnick)** – Former hacker turned security consultant and author of *The Art of Deception*.
- **[Bruce Schneier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Schneier)** – Cryptography and security expert, author of *Applied Cryptography*.
- **[Dan Kaminsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Kaminsky)** – Known for discovering critical DNS vulnerabilities.
- **[Mikko Hyppönen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikko_Hyppönen)** – Cybersecurity researcher, known for fighting malware and cybercrime.
- **[Katie Moussouris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Moussouris)** – Creator of Microsoft’s bug bounty program and advocate for ethical hacking.
- **[Charlie Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Miller_(security_researcher))** – Famous for hacking Apple devices and automotive cybersecurity research.
- **[Chris Nickerson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Nickerson)** – Founder of Lares and expert in red teaming and physical security.
- **[Tavis Ormandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tavis_Ormandy)** – Security researcher at Google Project Zero, known for vulnerability research.
- **[Marcus Hutchins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Hutchins)** – Stopped the WannaCry ransomware attack and cybersecurity researcher.
- **[Jon Erickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Erickson)** – Author of *Hacking: The Art of Exploitation*, expert in security and programming.
- **[Runa Sandvik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runa_Sandvik)** – Expert in privacy, anonymity, and Tor network security.
- **[Jayson E. Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayson_E._Street)** – Social engineering and physical penetration testing expert.
- **[Haroon Meer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haroon_Meer)** – Founder of Thinkst, creator of the Canary honeypot system.
- **[Peiter "Mudge" Zatko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peiter_Zatko)** – Former L0pht hacker, worked in DARPA and Twitter security leadership.
